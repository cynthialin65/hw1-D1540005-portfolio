工程 BOM 為什麼和成本核算有關？Why does the engineering BOM matter for cost accounting?

BOM 記錄產品由哪些主件與副件組成、各用多少，再結合各料件的單價，就能由最底層零件逐層向上累計出成品成本。

A BOM records which parent and child parts make up a product and in what quantity. Combined with component prices, cost can be rolled up level by level from the lowest parts to the finished product.

AI 在這個研究中扮演什麼角色？What role does AI play in this research?

研究構想是讓 AI 輔助成本估算、稽核與版本變化追蹤，支援管理決策；人的判斷與修正仍是核心。

The idea is for AI to assist cost estimation, auditing and version tracking to support management decisions, while human judgment and revision remain central.
